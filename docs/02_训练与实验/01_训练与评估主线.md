## 2. 当前最准确的论文主张

当前论文应该坚持的说法是：

1. `RGB gains are conditional rather than universal in low-resource CE-CSL translation.`
2. `Reliability-aware fusion improves the RGB-support branch.`
3. `The current model narrows the gap to pose-only, but does not yet show stable superiority over the stronger pose-only baseline.`

不应再写：

1. `state-of-the-art`
2. `consistently outperforms pose-only`
3. `RGB universally improves translation`
